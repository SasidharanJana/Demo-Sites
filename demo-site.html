Our Story

Started with one oven and a stubborn starter.

Ember & Wheat began as a single home oven and a sourdough starter that refused to die. Six years later, the process hasn't changed — only the size of the ovens.

Everything is still mixed, shaped, and baked on-site, before the neighborhood is awake.
